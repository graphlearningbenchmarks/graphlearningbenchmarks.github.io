abstract: 'Text-Attributed Graphs (TAGs), where each node is associated with text
  descriptions, are ubiquitous in real-world scenarios. They typically exhibit distinctive
  structure and domain-specific knowledge, motivating the development of Graph Foundation
  Models (GFM) that generalize across diverse graphs and tasks. Despite large efforts
  to integrate Large Language Models (LLMs) and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for TAGs,
  most methods suffer from decoupled architectures with two-stage alignment, limiting
  their synergistic potential. Even worse, existing methods assign out-of-vocabulary
  (OOV) tokens to graph nodes, leading to graph-specific semantics, token explosion,
  and incompatibility with task-oriented prompt templates, which hinders cross-graph
  and cross-task transferability. To address these challenges, we propose PromptGFM,
  a versatile GFM for TAGs grounded in graph vocabulary learning. PromptGFM comprises
  two key components: (a) graph understanding module, which explicitly prompts LLMs
  to replicate the finest GNN workflow within the text space, facilitating seamless
  GNN-LLM integration and elegant graph-text alignment; (b) graph inference module,
  which establishes a language-based graph vocabulary ensuring expressiveness, transferability,
  and scalability, enabling readable instructions for joint LLM fine-tuning across
  graphs and tasks. Extensive experiments demonstrate our superiority, especially
  effective transferability across diverse graphs and tasks. The code is available
  at.'
