abstract: Graph Convolutional Neural Networks (GCNNs) are the most recent exciting
  advancement in deep learning field and their applications are quickly spreading
  in multi-cross-domains including bioinformatics, chemoinformatics, social networks,
  natural language processing and computer vision. In this paper, we expose and tackle
  some of the basic weaknesses of a GCNN model with a capsule idea presented in and
  propose our Graph Capsule Network (GCAPS-CNN) model. In addition, we design our
  GCAPS-CNN model to solve especially graph classification problem which current GCNN
  models find challenging. Through extensive experiments, we show that our proposed
  Graph Capsule Network can significantly outperforms both the existing state-of-art
  deep learning methods and graph kernels on graph classification benchmark datasets.
