abstract: Graph neural network have achieved impressive results in predicting molecular
  properties, but they do not directly account for local and hidden structures in
  the graph such as functional groups and molecular geometry. At each propagation
  step, GNNs aggregate only over first order neighbours, ignoring important information
  contained in subsequent neighbours as well as the relationships between those higher
  order connections. In this work, we generalize graph neural nets to pass messages
  and aggregate across higher order paths. This allows for information to propagate
  over various levels and substructures of the graph. We demonstrate our model on
  a few tasks in molecular property prediction.
