Embedding Graphs on Grassmann Manifold

Bingxin Zhou, Xuebin Zheng, Yu Guang Wang, Ming Li, Junbin Gao

Abstract

Learning efficient graph representation is the key to addressing downstream tasks on graphs, such as node or graph property prediction. Given the non-Euclidean structural property of graphs, preserving the original graph data's similarity relationship in the embedded space needs specific tools and a similarity metric. This paper develops. The proposed to a Grassmann point of a low dimensional manifold through singular value decomposition (SVD).
