A*Net: A Scalable Path-based Reasoning Approach for Knowledge Graphs

Zhaocheng Zhu, Xinyu Yuan, Mikhail Galkin, Sophie Xhonneux, Ming Zhang, M. Gazeau, Jian Tang

Abstract

Reasoning on large-scale knowledge graphs has been long dominated by embedding methods. While path-based methods possess the inductive capacity that embeddings lack, their scalability is limited by the exponential number of paths. Here we present A*Net, a scalable path-based method for knowledge graph reasoning. Inspired by the A* algorithm for shortest path problems, our A*Net learns a priority function to select important nodes and edges at each iteration, to reduce time and memory footprint for both training and inference. The ratio of selected nodes and edges can be specified to trade off between performance and efficiency.
